How the type tester works

  1. Choose your language and difficulty from the settings menu or from the dropdown menu.
  2. Press the reset button to start a new run.
  3. Type the red word exactly as shown and press the space bar when it is complete.
  4. Each correct word increases your score and your typing speed is tracked live.
  5. Check your progress on the statistics page to see your past runs.
    6. If a word is not written correctly, it needs to be corrected before you can proceed.
